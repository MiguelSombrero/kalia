# Task 03: Kalia has its own name, icon and splash screen on a phone

- **Status:** needs-refinement
- **Iteration:** [10](../iteration-10.md)
- **Covers:** DW-1

## Why

The app still wears Expo's defaults: a placeholder icon and splash screen, and
a bundle identifier, `fi.kalia.app` (`mobile/app.json`), that nobody has
checked can be registered. A free Apple ID's Personal Team signs an app only
under an identifier no other team has claimed, so an identifier that is taken
blocks [task 04](04-free-apple-id-signing.md) outright — and an identifier
cannot change once an app is installed without the phone treating it as a
different app that has lost its data.

The first install on a real home screen is also the first time anyone sees
Kalia as an app rather than a simulator window.

## Scope

The app has a bundle identifier and Android package that a free Personal Team
can sign and that will not need to change later, a display name in both
locales, a home-screen icon and a splash screen drawn from Kalia's mark, and
the shipped image assets pass the raster-image check.

## Non-goals

- The mobile design language — type, colour use, density — which
  [iteration 11](../iteration-11.md) chooses from built alternatives; the
  icon here is the existing mark, not a new design.
- Store listing assets: parked for cost in
  [the backlog](../backlog.md#mobile-client).

## Constraints

- The mark is "a bottle pouring into foam on a teal tile"
  ([ADR-0072](../../adr/0072-the-mark-is-a-bottle-pouring-into-foam-on-a-teal-tile.md)),
  within [ADR-0065](../../adr/0065-imagery-icons-and-mark-specimen.md)'s
  imagery rules.
- `scripts/check-no-raster-images.mjs` fails a committed raster image that is
  not on its allowlist; a shipped asset goes on that allowlist, and a
  screenshot never does ([rule](../../../.claude/rules/no-screenshots.md)).
- `ios/` and `android/` are generated by `expo prebuild` and never committed
  ([ADR-0094](../../adr/0094-the-mobile-app-is-expo-as-a-development-build-with-generated-native-projects-and-no-paid-service.md));
  everything native comes from `app.json` and config plugins.
- An installed build keeps the identifier, icon and splash it shipped with; the
  [app-config check](../../architecture.md) cannot change them afterwards.
- Expo postdates model training — check docs.expo.dev at the SDK version in
  `mobile/package.json` for the icon and splash configuration, never memory.
- A splash or icon plugin not already in `mobile/package.json` is a new
  dependency — asked, not researched.

## Open questions

1. **The identifier.** Keep `fi.kalia.app` if a Personal Team can register it,
   or choose one now that a later paid account will also keep — and whether
   iOS and Android use the same one.
2. **The display name** under the icon: "Kalia" in both locales, or localised.
3. **The icon:** the web's mark as it stands, or a variant drawn for the
   home-screen grid and Android's adaptive mask — and whether a monochrome
   Android icon is wanted now.
4. **The splash screen:** the mark on its teal, the plain teal, or the app's
   background colour; and whether it follows dark mode before
   [iteration 11](../iteration-11.md) decides dark mode at all.
5. **Where the source lives:** the icon as SVG under version control and
   rendered to the PNGs Expo needs by a script, or the PNGs committed and
   allowlisted as they are.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The app installs on the iPhone and the Android emulator showing Kalia's
      icon, name and splash screen — seen by hand and described in the PR
- [ ] The identifier is confirmed registrable by a free Personal Team in the
      task 04 install, before any other build is installed on the phone
- [ ] `check-no-raster-images` passes with the shipped assets allowlisted, and
      its test covers an allowlisted mobile asset
- [ ] `make verify-fast` is green
